TEAM BRIEFING

Mapping ADII to the “End-to-End Agent Evaluation” requirement

What the requirement asks for, next to what our project actually does — including the evaluation loop that closes it.

REQUIREMENT

“Run a full evaluation cycle on a tool-using agent: capture traces, compare a baseline against an improved version, and make a release decision from the results — judging observable behavior only, never the model's internal reasoning.”

1

The agent's decision

REPAIR

The defect is real — here is the bounded fix.

NO_REPAIR

The state is legitimate; no action.

ESCALATE

Outside the agent's authority to decide.

2

Evaluation pipeline

investigate→ trace→ decision→ validate→ report
3

The evaluation loop — not just the pipeline

The pipeline above runs once per decision. The requirement's harder ask is the loop that runs around it — evaluate, diagnose what failed, improve, and prove the improvement on data the model never saw:

BASELINE (B0)→ EVALUATE→ FAILURE TAXONOMY→ IMPROVE→ P1→ FREEZE→ UNSEEN EVALUATION→ RELEASE / HOLD
WHAT IS SCORED, AT EVERY LAP
  • Investigation
  • Grounding
  • Authority
  • Validation
  • Final disposition
  • Cost / latency / bounds
